Dear Editors,

We submit "STAMPED principles for reproducible research objects" for consideration as an Article in Scientific Data.

Drawing on our years of experience building, sharing, and reviewing computational research objects, the manuscript formalizes seven principles (Self-containment, Tracking, Actionability, Modularity, Portability, Ephemerality, and Distributability: STAMPED) for structuring and managing research objects so that they can be verified, re-executed, and extended.
It addresses the mechanics of data organization, sharing, and reuse, and we believe it falls squarely within the Article scope.

STAMPED complements the FAIR Guiding Principles (https://doi.org/10.1038/sdata.2016.18), whose seminal paper was published in Scientific Data.
FAIR addresses discovery and reuse across repositories, while STAMPED describes principles for how the research object itself is built and run.

We are aware the submission guidelines advise against self-constructed acronyms in titles.
We have retained STAMPED in the title because introducing the acronym is the purpose of the paper, following the precedent of the FAIR principles cited above and the TRUST Principles (https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-020-0486-7), both published in Scientific Data with the acronym they introduce in the title.
We are happy to discuss alternatives if the editors prefer.

Corresponding author: Austin Macdonald, Dartmouth College (austin@dartmouth.edu, ORCID: 0000-0002-8124-807X).
